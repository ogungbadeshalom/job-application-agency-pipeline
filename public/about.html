Pitchr is a done-for-you job application service.

We find genuine remote openings, write a tailored resume for each one, submit the application, and track every send with proof. Someone has to do the work — so you don't have to.

What pitchr is

Built around three kinds of people.

🎯

Clients

the job seekers paying for the service

They log in, see their applications, their tailored resumes, their history, and the weekly read-out. They do not do the work.

🛠️

Workers

the people who do the work

They open a job, read the posting, customize the resume for it, and submit. The system pays them per confirmed application, capped weekly.

⚙️

Admin

the operators

Manages clients, workers, and the whole pipeline. Keeps quality high and everything moving on schedule.

The operating model

How it stays reliable.

📄

A customized resume per job

No copy-paste. Each application is tailored to that specific posting and matched to real skills — never invented ones.

🚫

No duplicate applications

The same company is never applied to twice, and duplicate listings are de-duplicated before they enter the queue.

✓

Accuracy is a hard rule

Resumes and applications contain only true, verifiable information about the client.

📸

Proof of work

Every submitted application has a record — confirmation, reference, and date/time. Some clients want screenshots; others just want the tally.

⏸️

Pause or stop anytime

No lock-in. Control over your campaign is always yours.

🔍

Where we scan

Greenhouse, Indeed, LinkedIn, ZipRecruiter, Monster, FlexJobs, We Work Remotely, RemoteOK, and more — the boards that actually list genuine remote work.

We track interviews. We never promise offers.

The client controls the application process; the employer controls the hiring decision. We report honestly on volume, quality, and progress.
