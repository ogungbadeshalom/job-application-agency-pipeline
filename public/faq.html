About the service

Do I apply to anything myself?

No. We find suitable openings, write a tailored resume for each one, submit the application, and track every send with proof. You log in, review, and interview.

How do I know you actually submitted the applications?

Every application ships with proof of submission — a timestamped confirmation record, reference, and the date and time. It's visible in your dashboard on every tier, so you never have to ask.

Which job boards do you scan?

Greenhouse, Indeed, LinkedIn, ZipRecruiter, Monster, FlexJobs, We Work Remotely, RemoteOK, Working Nomads, and more — the boards that actually list genuine remote work.

Can you guarantee I'll get a job or interviews?

No, and we never claim to. We control the application process; the employer controls the hiring decision. We guarantee volume, quality, and honest progress — interviews are the outcome we track and report, not a promise.

Prices & payment

How are plans priced?

Monthly subscriptions from $99 to $399 based on application volume — Basic (200), Standard (500), Best Value (750), and Ultimate (1,000). Custom plans are priced individually.

When do I pay?

Monthly, in advance. You pay before each service period begins, we confirm your targets, and work starts. There's no per-application billing.

Can I pause or cancel?

Yes — pause or stop anytime, no lock-in. Control over your campaign is always yours.

Quality & safety

Are the resumes accurate?

Always. Resumes and applications contain only true, verifiable information about you — we never invent qualifications or application answers.

Do you ever apply to the same company twice?

No. The same company is never applied to twice, and duplicate listings are de-duplicated before they enter the queue.

How do you handle my job-platform credentials?

We obtain your explicit authorization before any submission, follow each job platform's rules, and handle credentials securely. We never store them unnecessarily.
