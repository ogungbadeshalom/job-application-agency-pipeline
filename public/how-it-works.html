Home · How it works

We run the whole application process for you.

One pipeline, eight steps, running end-to-end. You set the target and watch the numbers go up — with proof on every send.

1

Onboarding & targeting

We confirm your target roles, locations, experience, qualifications, and preferences. This defines every search from here on.

before work begins
2

Board scanning

We sweep the job sites that list genuine remote work — Greenhouse, Indeed, LinkedIn, ZipRecruiter, We Work Remotely, and more — and match openings against your profile.

3

Approval & authorization

You approve the openings you want before anything is submitted. Control stays with you at every step.

4

Tailored resume

A fresh resume is written for each specific posting. Academic facts stay accurate to your background — never invented.

5

Submission

The application is completed and submitted on the job platform, matched to the posting's keywords and requirements.

6

Proof of submission

Every send gets a timestamped record — confirmation, reference, and date/time — visible in your dashboard. No asking, no guessing.

7

Weekly reporting

A plain-English report shows what was submitted, where, and how your search is tracking toward interviews.

8

Renewal

The next month's applications are queued and the next service period is paid in advance. Pause or stop anytime.

Proof of submission is in every tier.Each application carries timestamped evidence it was genuinely submitted — a confirmation record, reference, and the date and time.
