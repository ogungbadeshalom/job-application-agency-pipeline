How credits work

One credit. One real application.

No fine-print games — here is exactly what a credit buys and what happens to the ones you don't use.

🎯

What counts as a credit

One credit is one valid application: submitted with a confirmation record, one per company per role, duplicates removed automatically. Counts are “up to”, because good-fit vacancies vary month to month — we never send bad-fit applications just to hit a number.

🔁

Unused credits carry over

If we send fewer applications than your plan's credits in a month, the unsent ones are added to next month — as long as you continue for a second month. Your volume isn't lost to a slow job market week.

✉️

Same quality on every plan

Every credit gets the same work: a tailored resume and a cover letter for each application. Higher tiers add volume, priority, and service — not better output.

🤝

The Pitchr Promise

5 qualifying interviews or an accepted offer within 4 months — or we keep working for free. The market decides who gets hired; our promise is that we don't stop until you're in.

✅

No fantasy guarantees

We promise applications with proof, first application within 24 hours of onboarding, and weekly reports. What we will never do is promise you a job offer — anyone who guarantees the market's decision is selling you a fantasy.
