Privacy policy

How Pitchr collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

  • Contact details you provide (name, email, target roles, preferences).
  • Job-search information you share so we can tailor applications accurately.
  • Usage data from the client portal (what you view, reports you read).

What we never collect

  • We never store your job-platform credentials longer than needed to complete submissions, and we handle them securely.
  • We never invent or fabricate qualifications, employers, or application details.

How we use your data

  • To match suitable openings and tailor applications to your real background.
  • To deliver weekly reports and the live application tracker.
  • To bill your monthly subscription in advance.

Authorization & consent

We obtain your explicit authorization before submitting any application on your behalf. You can pause or stop the service at any time.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share limited details with the workers and systems needed to complete and track your applications, and only as required to provide the service.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact hello@pitchr.com.
