Terms of service

The terms that govern your use of Pitchr's done-for-you job application service.

Last updated: October 2026

1. The service

Pitchr assists clients with job applications by searching job boards, tailoring resumes, and submitting authorized applications on their behalf. We do not contact recruiters on the client's behalf.

2. Subscriptions & payment

  • Plans are monthly subscriptions paid in advance.
  • Application limits are per calendar month (e.g. up to 750 on Best Value).
  • The next month's payment is collected before the next service period begins.

3. No guarantee of offers or interviews

Pitchr controls the application process, not the employer's hiring decision. We do not guarantee job offers, interviews, or hiring outcomes. We report on volume, quality of applications, and honest progress. Interviews are tracked and reported as a measurable signal, not promised as a result.

4. Your responsibilities

  • Provide accurate information about your background and preferences.
  • Authorize applications before they are submitted.
  • Respond to requests for information that affect delivery.

5. Our reliability commitments

  • Accurate, verifiable information only — we never invent qualifications.
  • No duplicate applications to the same company.
  • Proof of submission with every application.

6. Pausing & cancellation

You can pause or cancel at any time. No lock-in. Prepaid service periods are completed unless otherwise agreed.

7. Authorization & security

We handle job-platform credentials securely, follow platform rules, and only submit with your explicit authorization.

8. Limitation of liability

Pitchr is not liable for outcomes you control or that depend on third parties — including employer hiring decisions, job-platform availability, or changes in a listing. We make reasonable efforts toward the application targets described but do not guarantee a specific number of completed applications in any given period.

9. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

10. Contact

Questions about these terms: hello@pitchr.com
